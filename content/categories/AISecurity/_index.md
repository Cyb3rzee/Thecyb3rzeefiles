When I started my journey in cybersecurity, I went straight into penetration testing. I did not really explore other paths. I committed to it and stayed loyal to it. Over time, I built my experience in web, API, and network penetration testing. That was my whole focus, and I thought that was where I would stay.

Then something changed.

I applied for an AI security program, and one of the requirements was to take the AI Security Fundamentals course on AI Security University. I took it, and honestly, I found it very interesting. Around that same time, someone in the CyberGirls group shared her journey into AI security and recommended the Blue Dot Impact course. 

I decided to try it out. I started the Future of AI course on Blue Dot Impact, and I have completed it now. I also went down a rabbit hole of watching YouTube videos and taking other courses. 

The more I learned, the more I realized something: I find AI security way more interesting and intriguing than traditional penetration testing.

Learning about things like Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) really blew my mind. There will come a time when AI can become smarter than humans and do things that people simply cannot do. Seeing how fast this tech is moving made me ask: how do we actually secure something like this?

Because of that, I made the decision to shift my focus. I am not completely throwing away my pentesting background, but I am putting it aside to focus heavily on AI security. 

People ask about governance and policy, but I do not think the policy side is for me right now. I have always found it hard to put what is in my head into writing or communicate policy stuff clearly. I lean much more toward the technical side, so technical AI security makes the most sense for me. But I am still open and learning as I go.

That is actually the reason I built this blog. I wanted a space where I can document what I am learning, share my transition, and figure things out in public. 

This is just day one of the shift, and I am excited to see where it takes me.
